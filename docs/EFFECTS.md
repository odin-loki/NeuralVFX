# More effects: steam and magic

Status: **done** (11 October 2026). Audience: dev, research, artists. Two effects that a game would want joined fire,
smoke and the explosion: **steam** (a vent's jet of white vapour) and **magic** (a spinning portal of violet to
white light). Both come from the project's own 2D fluid simulator with new sources, forces and drawing, were trained as
rollout effects with study D's recipe ([REPORT.md](REPORT.md) §6), and were tested as study D was: on held-out settings
and seeds, tracking from the true state and endless statistics against real runs and the nearest training flipbook.
A scene script shows them with study D's fire and explosion (§6).
